Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The Curious Minute is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The app requests permission to upload and manage videos on the authorized YouTube account. It does not access, collect or store data about any other users.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload and update the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
